How to export activities from Garmin Connect
  1. Go to connect.garmin.com/modern/activities
  2. Scroll down to load all your activities
  3. Click the Export CSV button in the top-right
  4. Upload the downloaded CSV file here

How to export health data from Garmin Connect
  1. Go to Garmin Connect on your desktop
  2. Navigate to Health & Performance → Reports
  3. Select each metric (Steps, Heart Rate, Sleep, Stress)
  4. Set date range and click the Export icon
  5. Upload each downloaded CSV file here
